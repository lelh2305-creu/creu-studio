## Tại Sao Content Marketing Quan Trọng Với Studio Nội Thất?

**Khách hàng cao cấp chọn studio bằng mắt.** Họ scroll Instagram, xem website, đánh giá aesthetic, trước khi quyết định liên hệ. Nếu visual của studio không đủ đẹp và nhất quán, họ không liên hệ dù studio thực tế làm rất tốt.

**Hành trình quyết định dài.** Thiết kế nội thất là quyết định lớn: khách hàng thường follow và quan sát một studio 3-6 tháng trước khi liên hệ. Content đều đặn giúp studio luôn hiện diện trong tâm trí họ.

**Portfolio sống trên social tốt hơn portfolio tĩnh trên website.** Website cập nhật 6 tháng một lần, Instagram cập nhật hàng tuần. Khách hàng thấy studio đang active, đang làm dự án mới, đang có kết quả thực tế.

# Content Marketing for Interior Design Studios: How to Get Great Clients Without Sacrificing Design Time

Good designers usually hate doing marketing. Not because they're lazy — but because they know how to do what they're good at: design. Sitting down to write Instagram captions or manage a content calendar isn't their strength, and it's usually pushed to the bottom of the priority list.

The result: a beautiful design studio with an empty social media presence, an outdated website, and fewer clients than it deserves.

The solution isn't for designers to learn marketing. The solution is **systematising content and outsourcing execution**.

---

## Why Does Content Marketing Matter for Interior Studios?

**Premium clients choose studios with their eyes.** They scroll Instagram, check websites, evaluate aesthetics — before deciding to make contact. If the studio's visuals aren't beautiful and consistent, they won't reach out even if the studio actually does excellent work.

**The decision journey is long.** Interior design is a major decision — clients often follow and observe a studio for 3-6 months before contacting. Consistent content keeps the studio present in their minds.

**A living portfolio on social beats a static website portfolio.** Websites update every 6 months — Instagram updates every week. Clients see the studio is active, working on new projects, producing real results.

---

## Content System for Interior Studios

### Core content (make once, use forever)
- Professional photography for 5-10 best projects
- Walkthrough videos for 2-3 flagship projects
- Studio introduction, design philosophy, team articles

### Regular content (monthly)
- Instagram/Facebook posts about ongoing projects
- Behind-the-scenes: process from sketch to completion
- Detail shots: materials, small corners, special lighting
- Short design tips: how to choose paint colors, furniture arrangement

### SEO content (2-4 articles/month)
- "Small bedroom interior design: 10 space-maximising ideas"
- "Interior design cost for a 70m2 apartment in HCMC 2026"
- "Interior trends 2026: hottest colors and materials"
